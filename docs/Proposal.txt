Proposed Contribution

We present a decoder-level reformulation for multimodal trajectory forecasting that replaces direct coordinate prediction with latent-conditioned kinematic generation followed by differentiable map-constrained refinement.

Technical Formulation (clear and complete)

Given observed context, we model trajectory generation as a structured process with three variables:

Latent variable (Z): captures high-level behavioral uncertainty (e.g., intent, style)
Kinematic parameters (E): define a motion generator (e.g., velocity, acceleration, turn rate)
Trajectory (Y): produced via deterministic rollout

The model operates in four stages:

Latent inference: infer a distribution over latent variables conditioned on scene context
Expert parameterization: map latent variables to parameters of a kinematic motion model
Analytical rollout: generate trajectories using closed-form motion equations
Map-constrained refinement: adjust trajectories via differentiable optimization over a map-derived distance field
What is New (precise differentiation)
Output space reparameterization
The model predicts motion parameters instead of coordinates, shifting trajectory generation from unconstrained regression to structured dynamical synthesis.
Functional role of latent variables
Latent variables do not directly generate trajectories; they control the motion generator, introducing a structured and interpretable mechanism for multimodality.
Deterministic kinematic rollout as the decoder
Trajectories are obtained through analytical integration of motion equations, ensuring temporal consistency and bounded dynamics without requiring the network to learn these properties.
Post-generation constraint enforcement via differentiable refinement
We incorporate a map-based optimization layer that projects trajectories onto feasible regions using gradient-based updates, decoupling constraint satisfaction from the learned model.
Why this formulation is justified

Direct coordinate prediction entangles:

intent
dynamics
uncertainty

within a single mapping, which often results in:

physically inconsistent trajectories
unstable multimodal predictions
degraded long-horizon behavior

By explicitly factorizing:

intent (latent)
motion mechanism (kinematics)
environmental constraints (map refinement)

the proposed formulation introduces a structured inductive bias aligned with the underlying generative process of motion.

Positioning relative to prior work
Latent-variable models capture multimodality but typically generate trajectories directly.
Motion-primitive and kinematic models impose structure but lack flexible latent conditioning.
Map-aware models incorporate constraints but usually only as input features or loss terms.

Our contribution is the integration of these components into a single, coherent generation pipeline, where:

latent variables parameterize motion primitives
trajectories are generated via physics-based rollout
constraints are enforced through a differentiable optimization layer
Scope and limitations (explicit)
The expressivity of the model depends on the chosen set of kinematic experts
The refinement step introduces additional computational cost
Interaction modeling is handled at the latent inference stage and not explicitly in the rollout
Concise Claim

We introduce a structured generative decoder that produces trajectories via latent-conditioned kinematic motion and enforces feasibility through differentiable map-based refinement, improving physical consistency and interpretability without relying on direct coordinate regression.
